About this portal

The Open Extragalactic Globular Cluster Portal is a community resource that compiles spectroscopically confirmed extragalactic globular clusters (GCs) alongside space-based photometric candidates from HST and JWST imaging. The primary goal is to provide a clean, LSST-ready training set for photometric classification of GC candidates in the upcoming Rubin Observatory Legacy Survey of Space and Time.

Objects are classified as confirmed when a radial velocity and/or spectral index measurement places them kinematically or chemically consistent with GC membership of the host galaxy. Candidates are selected on morphological and colour grounds from high-resolution space imaging and have not yet received a spectroscopic follow-up.

Photometry is provided in the SDSS / LSST filter system (u, g, r, i, z) where available, supplemented by HST passbands (F606W, F814W, F475W). Magnitudes are on the AB system. Coordinates are J2000.0 (ICRS).
